Steps

  1. Upload attendanceDrop the monthly attendance workbook. If it has multiple sheets, the "Modify" / "Final" tab is used automatically.
  2. Choose projectPick the site preset — it sets the wage zone, Sal Cal Days rule and Pay Days rule.
  3. Auto-map columnsEmp Code, Name, Designation, DOJ, PR / WO / AB / OT days are detected from the header row.
  4. Classify skillDesignation is mapped to Unskilled / Semi / Skilled / Highly Skilled.
  5. Apply wage ratesCentral CLC or state-zone Basic + VDA daily rates for the selected project are applied.
  6. Compute registerBasic, VDA, OT, PF, ESI, PT and Net Amount are calculated per employee.
  7. Review on screenCheck the table, then download the full-format Excel register.

Formulas Used

Basic / VDA (actual)

OT Amount

Total Earning
Basic + VDA + Incentive + OT Amt
Total Earning w/o OT
Total Earning − (Incentive + OT Amt)
PF
ROUND(15000 × 12% / Sal Cal Days × Pay Days)
PF Earning
ROUND(15000 / Sal Cal Days × Pay Days)
ESI
ROUNDUP(IF(TE(Th) > 21000, 0, TE × 0.75%))
Net Amount
Total Earning − Total Deduction
