Reconcile & Download

Step 4–5

For every employee's A day, one spare OT cell (PP, or WP if no PP is left) is spent to convert it to present — PP→P, or WP→WO (never WP→P, which would inflate PR DAYS). PR DAYS is capped at 27/month for 31-day months, 26 otherwise. Any employee with a matched note (see above) is skipped entirely — this overrides everything else.
